About Statue of Unity

Introduction

The Statue of Unity is the world's tallest statue, standing at a height of 182 metres (597 feet). It is located in the Narmada Valley near Ekta Nagar in the state of Gujarat, India.

Dedicated to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

The statue depicts Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (1875–1950), an Indian politician and freedom fighter. He was the first Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister of independent India. He played a major role in the political integration of India.

Location

The Statue of Unity is situated on the Narmada River at Sadhu Bet, near Ekta Nagar in Gujarat. It faces the Sardar Sarovar Dam and is approximately 100 kilometres southeast of Vadodara city.

Construction and Development

The project was first announced in 2010 by Narendra Modi, who was then the Chief Minister of Gujarat. Construction began in October 2013 and was carried out by the Indian company Larsen & Toubro.

The total construction cost was approximately ₹27 billion (US$422 million). The statue was designed by renowned Indian sculptor Ram V. Sutar.

Inauguration

The Statue of Unity was inaugurated by Narendra Modi, then Prime Minister of India, on 31 October 2018. The inauguration took place on the occasion of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's 143rd birth anniversary.
